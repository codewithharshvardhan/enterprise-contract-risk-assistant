CONSULTING STATEMENT OF WORK

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into as of April 10, 2026, pursuant to the Master Consulting Agreement dated January 5, 2026 (the "MCA") between Beacon Hill Financial Group ("Client") and Iversen Strategy Partners LLC ("Consultant"). Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meanings given in the MCA.

1. ENGAGEMENT AND SCOPE
Consultant shall provide strategic advisory services to Client in connection with Client's planned expansion into the Southeast regional market, including: (a) market entry analysis; (b) competitive landscape assessment; (c) a phased go-to-market recommendation; and (d) a final presentation to Client's executive leadership team.

2. DELIVERABLES AND SCHEDULE
Consultant shall deliver: (a) a market entry analysis report within four (4) weeks of the Effective Date; (b) a competitive landscape assessment within six (6) weeks of the Effective Date; (c) a phased go-to-market recommendation within nine (9) weeks of the Effective Date; and (d) a final executive presentation within ten (10) weeks of the Effective Date.

3. FEES AND EXPENSES
Client shall pay Consultant a fixed fee of $185,000 for the engagement described in this SOW, payable in three (3) installments: $65,000 upon execution of this SOW, $60,000 upon delivery of the go-to-market recommendation, and $60,000 upon completion of the final executive presentation. Client shall reimburse Consultant for reasonable, pre-approved travel expenses incurred in connection with this engagement, invoiced monthly with supporting documentation. Payment is due within thirty (30) days of invoice.

4. TERM
This SOW commences on April 10, 2026 (the "Effective Date") and terminates upon Consultant's completion of all deliverables described in Section 2, unless earlier terminated in accordance with Section 8 of the MCA.

5. PERSONNEL
Consultant shall assign Elena Bautista as lead engagement partner and shall not substitute the lead engagement partner without Client's prior written consent, except in the case of the lead partner's departure from Consultant's firm.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Consultant acknowledges that in the course of this engagement it will have access to Client's confidential financial projections, market strategy, and internal analyses. Consultant agrees to hold such information in strict confidence in accordance with Section 9 of the MCA, and shall not disclose it to any third party, including competing clients, without Client's prior written consent.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All deliverables produced by Consultant under this SOW shall be considered "work made for hire" and shall be owned exclusively by Client upon full payment of all fees due under this SOW. Consultant retains ownership of its pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, and templates used to produce the deliverables, and grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use such pre-existing materials solely as incorporated into the deliverables.

8. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, or partner of Client. Nothing in this SOW shall be construed to create an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Consistent with Section 11 of the MCA, Consultant's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this SOW shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client under this SOW, except in cases of gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality obligations.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This SOW is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, consistent with Section 14 of the MCA.

11. INCORPORATION OF MCA TERMS
Except as expressly modified herein, all terms and conditions of the MCA, including its provisions on termination, dispute resolution, insurance, and assignment, are incorporated into this SOW by reference and shall govern the engagement described herein.
