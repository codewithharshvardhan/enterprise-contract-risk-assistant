MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made between Prairie Wind Logistics LLC ("Client") and Ferro & Vance Consulting Group ("Provider"). Provider will perform certain services for Client as described below.

1. SERVICES
Provider shall provide supply-chain optimization consulting services to Client, including analysis of Client's warehouse routing and delivery scheduling processes. Specific deliverables and timelines will be discussed between the parties as the engagement progresses.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
Client shall pay Provider for services rendered. Invoices will be issued periodically and are due upon receipt. Provider reserves the right to adjust its rates as it deems appropriate for ongoing work.

3. TERM
This Agreement begins on the date of signature and continues until the services are completed to Provider's satisfaction.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
The parties agree to keep confidential any information shared in connection with this engagement that is marked "Confidential."

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Any reports, analyses, models, or other work product created by Provider in the course of this engagement shall remain the sole property of Provider. Client may use such work product internally for its own business purposes during the term of this Agreement.

6. WARRANTY
Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to perform the services described above.

7. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party shall be responsible for delays caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control.

8. GENERAL
This Agreement, together with any statements of work executed by both parties, represents the understanding between Client and Provider regarding the services described herein. Either party may propose amendments in writing.
